## P2.6a: the engine, the selector, the nested evaluator

1. **One generic primitive.** Everything below calls one function: fit a recipe on a population D (row ids, labels, groups) under a fold assignment of D, and predict D's held-out rows plus a declared set of extra rows (outer validation rows, test rows). `engine.run_recipe_cv` already takes arbitrary rows, folds and "test" rows, so the primitive is a thin layer (`songpop/phase2/populations.py`) that builds D's raw rows, its folds and the prediction rows, and returns a population bank keyed by the population's identity. Production CV is the special case D = the 3,863 eligible rows with the saved folds.
2. **Folds of an internal population.** Five `StratifiedGroupKFold` folds on D's rows sorted by row id, with primary groups, `shuffle=True` and `random_state` a hash of (protocol name, level, outer coordinates, split seed), so they depend only on D and the protocol coordinates, never on the candidate. Every learned candidate on the same D shares them. A check that every group is wholly inside one fold.
3. **Fit contexts.** Encoders that use an inner split (the date target encodings) get a `FitContext` whose split seed and fold carry the level, so an inner fit never reuses an outer fit's inner split by accident; the receipt records it.
4. **The population-keyed cache.** A cache entry is keyed by the hash of: the data files, the fitting row ids and labels, the groups, the ordered prediction row ids, the fold assignment, every fit-context seed, the recipe hash, the estimator seeds, the source manifest and the package versions. Stored as a bank-like npz plus JSON under `artifacts/phase2/cache/` (ignored by git) or in memory; an entry from another fitting population is refused on load (its key is recomputed from the request, not trusted from the file).
5. **Learned procedures (plan section 7.2).** For each of the 15 outer folds (r, k) of production CV: D = the outer training part; for each channel, the inner out-of-fold predictions on D from the five meta folds (estimator seeds averaged first); the combiner fitted on that matrix and D's labels; the channel's outer-fold model, which is exactly the production bank's fit on D, predicts the outer validation rows and the test rows (taken from the channel's production bank rather than refitted, after checking that the bank was fitted with the same recipe, seeds and sources); the combiner maps them. The result is an ordinary prediction bank (oof, test_by_fold, fold_auc) with its own artifact schema: channels, atomic recipes, meta-fold assignments and their hashes, objective, regularization, clipping, the 15 fitted weight vectors or coefficient sets, scaler statistics, receipts. Deployment averages the 15 procedures' test predictions, which is what `test_mean` already is.
   - Constrained weights: nonnegative, summing to one, minimizing the mean binary log loss (equal row weights) of the clipped convex combination (clip 1e-6) plus lambda times the squared distance to equal weights; SciPy SLSQP from equal weights, `ftol` 1e-12, `maxiter` 1000; failure (status not 0, a violated constraint beyond 1e-9, a non-finite prediction) raises; tiny negative weights from the solver are clipped to zero and renormalized, the change recorded. lambda in {0, 0.01, 0.1}.
   - Stacker: probabilities clipped to [1e-6, 1 - 1e-6], logits, `StandardScaler` fitted on the meta-training matrix, `LogisticRegression(C, penalty l2, intercept, lbfgs, max_iter 1000)`, no original features, no class weights; C in {0.01, 0.1, 1}.
   - All six settings share the meta-features of each outer fold, computed once.
6. **Selector (`songpop/phase2/selection.py`).** `select_candidate(banks, menu)`: refuses a bank that is not complete on the declared split seeds or has another population; ranks by unrounded mean fold AUC; the tie band 0.001 below the maximum; tie-breaks: fewer unique atomic recipes, fewer supervised feature transformations (date target encodings, date counts, learned combiners' channels counted by rule), fewer learned combiners, the lower `complexity_rank`, the candidate id. It receives banks only, never outer predictions, a public score or a global bank in the nested use.
7. **Nested evaluator (`songpop/phase2/nested.py`, run by P2.7).** Section 8 as written: three outer folds (split seed 20261101) saved in `artifacts/phase2/splits/`; inside each outer training part O, inner folds with split seeds 155, 2026 and 1029 rebuilt on O; every menu atomic recipe fitted inner-fold by inner-fold, predicting the inner validation rows and the outer validation rows; learned candidates rebuild their meta folds inside each inner training part; `select_candidate` on the inner banks; the selected candidate's inner-fold average predicts the outer validation rows; separately the selected procedure refitted on all of O; the anchor's fold-average prediction; the fallback and deployment decisions applied after all three outer folds, in that order. The reduced path of section 8 is declared and chosen from a measured projection before any outer score is printed.
8. **Isolation tests that fail on a wrong implementation.** Label isolation at every level: change the labels of rows outside a fit's population (the outer validation rows, the inner validation rows, the test rows) and every fit's predictions on its own population are unchanged, at the outer, inner and meta levels; a cache entry from another population refused; meta out-of-fold coverage exactly once per row of D with whole groups excluded; a duplicate channel or a member corrupted in a compensating way refused; full-refit artifacts kept apart from fold averages. Synthetic inputs and the anchor only; no candidate scores in P2.6a.
9. **Cost pilot.** On the anchor and one cheap second channel: one outer fold of a learned procedure and one outer fold of the nested evaluator, timed, extrapolated to the plan's 3.2 C_all + 16.2 C_L formula with measured C values; no score printed.

## P2.6b: the pool, the procedures, the menu

10. **H\*, the pool and the cap.** Exactly plan section 7.1: H\* the strongest eligible standalone recipe across P2.2, P2.3, P2.8, P2.4 and P2.5 by unrounded mean fold AUC (ties to the lower recipe id); per family its standalone winner and its contribution winner against H\* (blends at 0.9/0.1 and 0.75/0.25 recomputed against H\* on identical folds); the pool: the three strongest family representatives, up to two more by contribution to H\*, the anchor, up to two further configurations of the leading family within 0.002 of its leader by a fixed ranking; at most eight atomic recipes, H\* included. The rule is saved as a script and its output, deterministic from the log and banks, before any procedure score exists.
11. **Seed policies first.** Every stochastic classical pool recipe is refitted with estimator seeds 0, 1 and 2 before procedures are scored; deterministic ones (kNN, and any whose seeds give bit-identical predictions, shown by a check) once; the anchor keeps seed 0 and its three-seed version is a separate candidate; TabM with seeds 0, 1, 2 if the measured cost fits, else seed 0 declared before its score.
12. **The enumerated manifest (at most 32 procedures)**, frozen before evaluation: the equal averages of the two and three strongest channels and of every channel; the 0.9 and 0.75 contribution blends of each challenger with H\*; the equal within-family average; constrained weights with lambda in {0, 0.01, 0.1}; the stacker with C in {0.01, 0.1, 1}. Channels: one per family representative, the within-family average replacing its family's single channel; the anchor a standalone candidate only.
13. **Menu (at most 12) and the freeze.** The anchor, its three-seed version, the selected standalone leaders, the within-family average, the best fixed blend, the best constrained-weight procedure, the best stacker, the rest by the section 11 rule; complexity ranks fixed before evaluation (by the tie-break order: atomic recipes, supervised transformations, learned combiners, then a declared kind order); `configs/phase2/menu.json` with exact recipes, seeds, weights, objectives, meta-fold policy, the selection rule and the nested protocol, hashed.
