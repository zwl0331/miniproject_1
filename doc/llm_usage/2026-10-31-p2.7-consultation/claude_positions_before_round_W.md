# Claude's positions on the P2.7 driver, written before round W (2026-10-31, 06:50 PDT)

Author: Claude Fable 5.1 (Claude Code), the primary agent. Written before the round W prompt was sent, so that Codex's design is independent of it.

1. Scope. One driver, `analysis/p27_execute.py`, run as separate stage processes like `analysis/p26_execute.py`: `measure`, `smoke`, `run`, `decide`, `replay`. The engine (`songpop/phase2/nested.py`, `procedures.py`, `selection.py`, `engine.py`) is not edited: those files are recorded sources of the frozen banks, and the R2 review approved the evaluator as it stands.
2. Population and splits. The population is the eligible rows with their primary groups, as in the cost pilot (`procedures.Population(ctx.rows, ctx.data.y, ctx.groups, "production")`). The outer assignment is `nested.outer_folds(pop)` (three StratifiedGroupKFold folds, seed 20261101), saved once as `artifacts/phase2/splits/nested_outer_v1.csv` (row id, outer fold) with its sha256 recorded; every later stage loads the saved file, checks it equals a recomputation and passes it as `fold=`.
3. Measure, score-free, with nothing else running. The 8 menu atom recipes (`menu.p27_reservation.menu_atom_runs`) are each cross-fitted on the 15 production folds through `procedures.atomic_cv` with an in-memory cache, timing each; C_all is their sum and C_L the sum over the 6 learned-channel atoms. Nothing new is scored: these are the production fits whose scores are already logged. The projection follows the plan (3.2 C_all + 16.2 C_L) and, as a cross-check, the pilot's multipliers (3.04 C_all + 14.16 C_L); build plus empty-cache replay is twice the projection, with a 25% margin; the path is full if that is at most eight hours, else reduced (the frozen `nested_protocol.reduced_path`). The stage records load averages before and after, the process list check (no other project process), the times, both projections and the decision in `artifacts/phase2/p27/measure.json`, before any outer score exists; `run` refuses to start without that file and takes its path from it.
4. Smoke. `nested.run` on the real executable menu over the first 400 eligible rows with a throwaway in-memory cache and a scratch manifest tag, then `nested.decide` on it; it asserts shapes, coverage and that every stage of the real recursion runs (real recipes, real fits); its numbers are not printed or kept, since they are not an evaluation of anything.
5. Run. `nested.executable_menu(menu)`; the anchor from `nested_protocol.anchor`; the reduced candidate, if that path, from `production_selection.selected`; the source manifest hash as the engine's runs record it (`banks.source_manifest`), `runs.packages_for` as `packages_of`; the cache as an on-disk namespace `artifacts/phase2/cache/p27_build/` (ignored by git) so that an interrupted build resumes from its own fits with identical request keys. The result (outer assignment, per fold: the selected id, the selection details, fold-average, full-refit and anchor predictions, validation row ids) is saved as `artifacts/phase2/p27/result_build.npz` plus a JSON of the non-array parts, with sha256s; a `nested` record goes to `artifacts/phase2/experiments.jsonl` (task P2.7, path, menu sha256, split sha256, manifest hash, cache hits and misses, elapsed time, result hashes). The stage prints the per-fold selection and its inner tie set (inner scores are permitted) and no outer score.
6. Decide. `nested.decide(result, pop)` plus the report the plan asks for: the three outer fold AUCs and their mean for the selection's fold average, its full refit and the anchor; the pooled outer out-of-fold AUC (the three folds' fold-average predictions concatenated in row order); the paired differences against the anchor and refit minus fold average; the selected candidate per fold; a group bootstrap of the pooled outer predictions (primary groups resampled with replacement, 2,000 resamples, seed from protocol coordinates) for the two paired differences, with the caveat that it is conditional on the realized selections and does not rerun the selection. Written to `artifacts/phase2/p27/decision.json` and printed; a `nested_decision` record in the log. The reduced path reports the fixed candidate's and the anchor's outer AUCs and decides nothing.
7. Replay. `nested.run` again with the saved assignment and a fresh, empty namespace `artifacts/phase2/cache/p27_replay/` (refused if it exists and is not empty); every array compared with the build within 1e-12, the selections exactly; the comparison written to `artifacts/phase2/p27/replay.txt`. It runs after the build, not beside it.
8. Order and timing. Positions, round W, driver, round X on the draft, fixes, smoke, measure with nothing else running, then run, replay, decide. Projection from the logged times under load: build 3.4 hours; clean times should be lower. Kaggle closes Monday 17:00 PST; phase 3 needs Sunday evening at the latest.
9. Records. Finding 030 (draft) with the outer results; `STATE.md` at each stage; one commit per stage; the P2.7 result gets its R1 with phase 3's selection evidence.
10. Risks. A crash mid-build: resume from the namespace. A projection above eight hours: the reduced path by the frozen rule unless Sam raises the reservation. Thread settings as in production (the families' own). Memory: the per-request arrays are small (inner out-of-fold vectors over about 2,575 rows and fold predictions over about 1,288 rows).
11. Decisions only Sam can make: raising the eight-hour reservation if the measured projection exceeds it.
