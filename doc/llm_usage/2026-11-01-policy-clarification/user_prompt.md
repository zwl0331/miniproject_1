Hey everyone,

Hope you are having fun with MP1! Just a reminder that no external data should be involved in your pipeline. This means:
1. No training on data obtained from the internet or sampled from a foundation model (which is trained on external data). However, augmentation based entirely on the provided data is allowed.
2. No model selection/validation based on external data, and please do not try to obtain the test labels.
Please do reach out for corner cases. After the competition ends we will check for reproducibility so please compete fairly :)
